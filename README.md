# Juliusgamespace

Survive the apocalypse in a brutal 2D world. Gather resources, craft weapons, build your shelter, and fight off endless threats. Every choice determines whether you live or die.

## Features

- **Resource Gathering** - Scavenge for materials in a harsh environment
- **Crafting System** - Create weapons and tools to enhance your survival
- **Base Building** - Construct and defend your shelter
- **Combat** - Face endless threats and enemies
- **Survival Mechanics** - Every decision impacts your chances of survival
